## Getting In: Gates, Entry & What to Bring

### What Time Do the Gates Open on Gameday?
**Q: What time do the gates open? / What time do the gates open on gameday? / how early can i get into the stadium / When can I get into the stadium? / What time can I enter? / When do the doors open? / I have a club seat, can I get in earlier? / How early do they let you in?**
Gates open 90 minutes before kickoff for all standard tickets. Club and Suite ticket holders can enter earlier — the East or West Club entrance opens 2 hours before kickoff.

- Standard tickets: main gates (Ford, Phillips 66, Kroger, or Xfinity) open 90 minutes before kickoff.
- Club or Suite tickets: East or West Club entrance opens 2 hours before kickoff.
- Each main gate has an ADA screening line and elevator access.
- Note: parking lots open earlier than the stadium gates. Lot hours are a separate policy — see "What Time Do Parking Lots Open."
- For a gate issue: visit a Guest Experience booth or text 832-241-0202.

### Re-Entry
- Re-entry is not permitted, except a limited policy for emergency medical conditions, reported at any Guest Experience booth.

### Bag Policy (Clear Bag)
**Q: What kind of bag can I bring? / whats the bag policy / Can I bring my backpack? / Is my purse allowed? / What size bag can I bring in?**
The NFL Clear Bag Policy is in effect at NRG Stadium. Approved bags are clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12" x 6" x 12", a one-gallon clear freezer bag, or a small clutch up to 4.5" x 6.5". Backpacks and non-clear bags larger than a small clutch are not allowed.
- These dimensions apply to bags only. They do not make any otherwise-prohibited item allowed.
- Full policy: nfl.com/legal/clear-bag-policy.

### Outside Food, Drinks and Coolers Are NOT Allowed
**Q: Can I bring my own food and drinks? / can i bring a cooler in / Are coolers allowed? / Can I bring a soft sided cooler? / Can I bring snacks into the game? / Can I bring my own water bottle?**
Outside food and beverages are not allowed inside the stadium. Coolers are not allowed inside the stadium.

- Coolers of every kind are prohibited — soft-sided and hard-sided alike. A soft-sided cooler or cooler bag is NOT permitted even if it would fit within the clear bag dimensions. The clear bag size limits never make a cooler allowable.
- Outside alcohol is not allowed.
- For medically necessary food or drink items, email fanfeedback@houstontexans.com.

### Drones
- Drones, "Unmanned Aircraft Systems," and other remotely controlled aircraft or ground vehicles are strictly prohibited at NRG Stadium and NRG Park.
- Anyone possessing or operating one is subject to confiscation of the item, removal from NRG Park, and possible legal action.

### Strollers & Car Seats Policy
- Strollers are allowed in the stadium, but the Houston Texans and NRG Stadium kindly request that guests refrain from bringing them into the stadium due to potential tripping hazards. However, there is limited stroller storage available at our guest experience booths if needed and they cannot be brought into the stands.
- Car seats are allowed in the stadium, but they cannot be brought into the stands and have to be checked at a Guest Experience booth.

### What You Can Bring Into the Stadium
**Q: Can I bring my camera? / Can I bring binoculars? / am i allowed a camera with a zoom lens / Can I bring a blanket? / Is an umbrella allowed? / Can I bring my iPad? / Can I bring medicine in?**
Cameras, binoculars, blankets, small umbrellas, iPads, and medicine are all allowed into NRG Stadium. Small cameras with lenses up to 3 inches (3") are permitted after inspection, with no additional or detachable lenses, and camera cases up to 4.5" x 6.5". Binoculars and binocular cases up to 4.5" x 6.5" are allowed after inspection.
- Computers and iPads (cases must follow the clear bag policy).
- Blankets if carried alone.
- Small compact umbrellas that fit under a seat, after inspection.
- Medicine, and items like baby wipes and formula, inside an NFL approved clear bag.
- Full permitted and prohibited list: houstontexans.com/stadium/prohibited-items.

### What You Cannot Bring Into the Stadium (Prohibited Items)
**Q: Can I bring an air horn? / is a cowbell ok at the stadium / Are noisemakers allowed? / Can I bring a seat cushion? / Are laser pointers allowed? / Can I bring a selfie stick? / What items are banned? / Is a battery operated fan allowed?**
Noisemakers of every kind are not allowed inside NRG Stadium, including cowbells, bullhorns, air horns, and vuvuzelas. Seat cushions are not allowed, laser pointers are not allowed, and coolers and outside food and beverages are not allowed. The full list of prohibited items follows.
- Weapons and firearms are not allowed.
- Flag poles and sign poles are not allowed.
- Frisbees and beach balls are not allowed.
- Selfie sticks are not allowed.
- Fireworks are not allowed.
- Illegal substances are not allowed.
- Battery operated fans are not allowed.
- Segways, skateboards, and hoverboards are not allowed.
- Diaper bags are not allowed (see Diaper Bags & Baby Items).
- Drones and remote-controlled aircraft are not allowed.
- Any other items deemed by management, in its sole discretion, to be hazardous or not in the spirit of the event.
- There is no coat check and no bag storage at the stadium.
- Full list: houstontexans.com/stadium/prohibited-items.

### Diaper Bags & Baby Items
**Q: Can I bring a diaper bag? / im bringing my baby, is a diaper bag ok / What do I do with diapers and formula? / Can I bring a breast pump?**
Diaper bags are not permitted inside NRG Stadium. Baby wipes, formula, and diapers may be brought in as long as they are placed inside an NFL approved clear bag. A medically necessary breast pump is allowed in a clear bag.

### Signs, Banners & Posters
- Welcome if football-related and in good taste: not commercial, political, religious, or obscene; small enough for one person to hold; not affixed to anything; and not blocking sightlines or signage.
- Poles are not permitted.
- More info: houstontexans.com/game-day/teammate-central/a-z-guide.

## Food, Drink & Alcohol/Liquor

### Beer & Alcohol Sales — Location, Cutoff Time & Policy (Combined)
**Q: Where can I find a beer? / When do they stop selling beer? / Whens last call for beer at the stadium? / Last call for beer? / Beer sales cutoff time? / How late can I buy a drink? / How late can I buy a drink at the game? / What time do they stop selling alcohol? / What time do they stop selling alcohol during a game? / When does beer sales stop? / What time is last call?**
Alcohol and liquor sales at concession stands stop after the 3rd quarter. Beer concessions are available on every level of the stadium as well as with hawkers throughout the aisles serving Bud Light, Budweiser, and Modelo. You must be 21 or older to buy or consume alcohol in the stadium or lots. Outside alcohol is not allowed. All drinks must be emptied, finished, or thrown away before leaving, though empty souvenir cups may leave.

### Gluten Free & Gluten-Avoiding Food Options
**Q: Are there gluten free food options at the stadium? / celiac here, what can i eat at the game / Where do I find gluten free concessions? / Do you have gluten free food? / What can I eat if I can't have gluten?**
Gluten-avoiding food is available at NRG Stadium. Plain baked potatoes and chopped beef with no sauce are sold at sections 109, 129, 501, 515, 527, and 541, and gluten-avoiding peanuts and cotton candy are sold at all concession stands. These items are produced in areas that also handle gluten, so this is not a gluten-free kitchen.

### Kosher Food Is NOT Offered at NRG Stadium
**Q: Where's the kosher food stand? / do you have kosher options / I keep kosher, which stand do I go to? / Is there kosher food at NRG?**
Kosher options are not offered at NRG Stadium at this time. There is no kosher stand or kosher vendor. The concession guide is at houstontexans.com/game-day/concessions-and-merch.

### Snacks & Specialty Stands

## Stadium Services & Amenities

### First Aid Station locations
- First Aid stations are located at sections 120, 140, 301, 329, 525, and 551.
- Text our gameday text-line at 832-241-0202 for immediate assistance on game day.

### Restrooms, Baby Changing & Nursing
**Q: Is there a nursing room? / where can i breastfeed at the game / Where is the family restroom? / Where can I change my baby?**
Restrooms, including family and ADA accessible restrooms, are on every level of NRG Stadium, with drinking fountains nearby and baby changing tables in the family restrooms on every level (100 to 500). The Mothers Nursing and Breastfeeding Room is at section 133, behind the Guest Experience booth on the Field Level; it is a private room with comfortable chairs and dim lighting.
- A medically necessary breast pump may be brought in inside an NFL approved clear bag.
- To report a restroom issue: text 832-241-0202 with the location.

### Elevators & Escalators
- Elevators for guests with disabilities are at six locations: East and West VIP entrances, Northwest at Xfinity, Northeast at Kroger, Southeast at Phillips 66, and Southwest at Ford.
- Escalators are at all four gates; the East and West Club escalators are for Club and Suite ticket holders only.
- To report a broken elevator or escalator: text 832-241-0202.

### WiFi at the Stadium
**Q: Is there free WiFi in the stadium? / whats the wifi network at nrg / How do I connect to WiFi? / Is there internet at the game?**
Complimentary WiFi is available to all stadium guests courtesy of Xfinity. Select the network "xfinitywifi" on your mobile device to connect.

### Charging Your Phone
**Q: Where can I charge my phone at the stadium? / is there a phone charger i can borrow / Do you rent chargers, and does it cost anything? / My battery is dying, what do I do?**
Portable chargers can be checked out from any Reliant Kiosk, located at all four major entrance ramps on the 100, 300, and 500 levels.
- The rental is free through 9pm the day after the rental.
- After 9pm the following day, a $3 per day fee accrues, up to 7 days.
- If the charger is not returned within 7 days, the guest is charged $39.99 for the charger.
- The East and West Club also have charging tables at Sections 303, 316, 331, and 344.
- For charger issues on gameday: text 832-241-0202.

### Reporting a Gameday Issue
- To report an air conditioning, cleanliness, or car/vehicle issue on gameday: text 832-241-0202.

### Lost & Found and Lockers
**Q: I lost my jacket at the game, what do I do? / i left my phone in my seat / Where's lost and found? / I lost something at a previous game / Is there a lost and found number?**
For an item lost DURING a game, visit a Guest Experience booth. For an item lost at a PREVIOUS game, email lostandfound@nrgpark.com or call 832-667-1714, and wait for confirmation before coming to retrieve it. Items are held 30 days.
- Lockers: mobile lockers are in the BULLevard on the South side by the Dome Circle on Naomi and on the North side off NRG Parkway, for $20.00 before taxes.

### Lost Person or Child
**Q: I can't find my son, what do I do? / my kid is lost in the stadium / I got separated from my group / Someone in my party is missing**
If a child, friend, or member of your party is lost on gameday, visit any Guest Experience booth, tell the nearest staff member, or text the gameday textline at 832-241-0202 for assistance.
- Register kids for the Track Your Texan Program at a booth for a wristband with a parent's contact info.

### Roof Status & Shade
**Q: Will the roof be open? / is the roof gonna be closed sunday / Who decides if the roof is open? / When is the roof decision made?**
The roof decision is made 2 hours before kickoff and cannot be changed afterward; under NFL rules, once it is made the only person who can change it is the Game Referee. Many factors affect the decision, including chance of rain, cloud cover, and projected wind speed, not just temperature. For an upcoming game, the roof status is not known in advance, so fans should check back before the next home game.
- With the roof open, a noon kickoff usually shades the west side (this can reverse for later kicks).

### Smoking & Vaping
**Q: Where can I smoke at NRG Stadium? / can i vape at the game / Is there a smoking section inside? / Can I smoke inside the stadium?**
NRG Park is smoke free, including e-cigarettes and vaping, in seating areas and concourses. Smoking is only permitted in the designated smoking terraces, which are on all four sides of the Field Level, on the Club Level, and in each corner of the Upper Level. If one terrace is closed, another on the same level stays open.
- To report smoking near you: text 832-241-0202.

### Security, Conduct & Safety
- Police and security are stationed throughout the stadium.
- For a security matter, a fan conduct issue, or foul language: text 832-241-0202 or visit a Guest Experience booth (members: STM Headquarters at 132-133 or 544-545).
- In an evacuation, know your nearest exits and follow staff instructions.
- Fan Code of Conduct: static.clubs.nfl.com/image/upload/texans/ewm7ijjjtbbau0oa9gae.

### Wheelchairs & Wheelchair Escorts
**Q: Is there a wheelchair available? / do you have wheelchair assistance / My dad can't walk to our seats, can someone help? / Do you have wheelchair escorts?**
Complimentary wheelchair service is available for guests. Guests can receive a complimentary wheelchair escort to and from their seats or other areas within the stadium.
- To request one: notify a representative upon entry, or text the gameday textline at 832-241-0202.
- 832-241-0202 is the only number for gameday wheelchair and escort requests.

### Accessibility & Sensory Services
**Q: Do you have a sensory room? / is there a quiet room for sensory issues / My child gets overwhelmed by crowd noise, what's available? / Do you have assisted listening devices?**
NRG Stadium offers sensory and accessibility services. The KultureCity Sensory Room is across from the field-level Guest Experience booth at section 133, and KultureCity sensory bags can be checked out at each Guest Experience booth with a driver's license or credit card as a deposit. Assisted Listening Devices are available at any Guest Experience booth on the same deposit terms.
- Closed captioning is shown in the lower northwest and southwest corners of the bowl.
- Segways are not available.
- For accessible seating, call the Ticket Office at 832-667-2150.

### Pets Are NOT Allowed — Service Animals Only
**Q: Can I bring my dog to the game? / are pets allowed at nrg / I have a service dog, can he come in? / Are emotional support animals allowed?**
Pets and animals are not permitted in or on NRG Park property. The only exception is certified guide, signal, or service animals trained to aid guests with disabilities. Emotional support animals are not permitted.

### There Are NO ATMs at NRG Park — the Stadium Is 100% Cashless
**Q: Where's the nearest ATM? / can i pay cash at concessions / Is there an ATM in the stadium? / I only brought cash, what do I do? / Do you take credit cards?**
NRG Park is 100% cashless. There are NO ATM machines available anywhere at NRG Park. All major credit cards are accepted, including Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express.

**Q: Can I book a tour of NRG Stadium? / do you do stadium tours / How much is a stadium tour? / Can we see the locker room?**

- All tickets are mobile and load into your Account Manager when the schedule releases in May.

### Discounted Tickets Are NOT Offered
**Q: Do you offer any discounted tickets? / any ticket discounts available / Are there cheaper ticket options? / Is there a student or senior discount?**

There is no military discount and no military comp ticket program. To learn about the Texans' partnerships with local organizations — including the USO, the Wounded Warrior Project, the Lone Star Veterans Association, and Operation FINALLY HOME — visit houstontexans.com/community/programs/salute-to-service.

## Parking, Tailgating & Getting There

### Buying & Pricing Parking
**Q: How much does parking cost? / price for a single game parking pass / What would a full season parking pass run me? / How much is parking at NRG?**
Parking at NRG Stadium costs $25.00 to $85.00 for a single-game parking pass and $440.00 to $1,760.00 for a full-season parking pass, based on lot location and proximity to the stadium. All parking requires a purchased parking pass and it is recommended to buy in advance, though cashless parking may be available.
- Buy single-game parking passes on the NFL Ticket Exchange at ticketmaster.com/houston-texans-tickets/artist/821630.
- Cash lots around the stadium are not associated with NRG Park.
- More info: houstontexans.com/game-day/parking-tailgating.

### Managing Your Parking Pass
- Parking passes are mobile in the Texans App and can also be printed from a desktop, and they must be paid in full before transfer or sale.
- To view: in the app tap TICKETMASTER, sign in, tap the parking event, tap VIEW BARCODE.
- To forward: tap MORE, MANAGE MY ACCOUNT, sign in, tap the parking event, TRANSFER, select the pass, TRANSFER TO, enter info, TRANSFER TICKETS.
- To sell: tap EXPLORE, MANAGE MY TICKETS, sign in, tap the parking event, SELL, set your price, set payment, SELL TICKETS.
- Refunds aren't offered, but you can resell at am.ticketmaster.com/texans.
- Don't see your pass: call 832-667-2002, option 1.

### What Time Do Parking Lots Open and Close?
- All parking lots open four hours prior to kickoff.
- Platinum opens five hours prior to kickoff.
- Parking lots close one hour following the conclusion of the game.
- Note: these are LOT hours, not stadium gate times. Stadium gates open 90 minutes before kickoff (2 hours for Club and Suite) — see "What Time Do the Gates Open on Gameday?"

### ADA & Handicap Parking
**Q: I have a handicap placard, where do I park? / is ada parking free if i have a placard / Do I still need a parking pass if I have an accessible plate? / Where is handicap parking?**
ADA parking is available in every lot at NRG Park on a first-come, first-served basis, but it requires BOTH a pre-purchased parking pass AND a government-issued placard or license plate. An ADA placard alone does not grant access to a lot without a verified parking pass.
- On gamedays an ADA shuttle runs from Gate 2 to Hall E at NRG Center starting 1.5 hours before kickoff.
- Placard info: txdmv.gov/motorists/disabled-parking-placards-plates.

### Rideshare, Uber, Lyft & Taxi Pickup
**Q: Where do I get picked up for an Uber after the game? / wheres the uber pickup spot / Where is rideshare pickup? / Where do taxis pick up? / What's the Lyft pickup location?**
The rideshare and taxi pick-up and drop-off location is in Yellow Lot 35. All rideshares and taxis may drop off and pick up in the Yellow Lot.

### The Texans Do NOT Run a Shuttle or Limo Service
**Q: Is there a shuttle from downtown? / do the texans run a limo service / I booked a hotel shuttle, where does it drop off? / Is there a Texans shuttle bus?**
The Houston Texans do not currently offer a shuttle service and do not offer a limo service. There is no Texans-operated shuttle route or schedule. If you secure your own limo service or hotel shuttle, the drop-off location is on the West Side of the property in the Green Lot.

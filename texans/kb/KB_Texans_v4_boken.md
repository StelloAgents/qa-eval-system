## Getting In: Gates, Entry & What to Bring

Everything a fan needs before they reach the building — when they can get in, and what they can carry.

---

### Gate Times, When Fans Can Enter

**The policy:** Gates open 90 minutes before kickoff for all standard tickets. Club and Suite ticket holders can enter earlier, through the East or West Club entrance, which opens 2 hours before kickoff.

**Key details:**
- Standard tickets: main gates — Ford, Phillips 66, Kroger, or Xfinity — open 90 minutes before kickoff.
- Club or Suite tickets: East or West Club entrance opens 2 hours before kickoff.
- Each main gate has an ADA screening line and elevator access.
- Parking lots open earlier than the stadium gates and are a separate policy. See "Parking Lot Hours."
- For a gate issue: visit a Guest Experience booth or text 832-241-0202.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks what time gates open, how early they can get into the stadium, when doors open, or whether a club seat gets them in sooner.

---

### Re-Entry, Not Permitted

**The policy:** Re-entry is not permitted at NRG Stadium. The only exception is a limited policy for emergency medical conditions, which must be reported at any Guest Experience booth.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks about leaving and coming back, stepping out to their car, or tailgating at halftime.

---

### Bag Policy, NFL Clear Bag Rules

**The policy:** The NFL Clear Bag Policy is in effect. Approved bags are clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags up to 12" x 6" x 12", a one-gallon clear freezer bag, or a small clutch up to 4.5" x 6.5". Backpacks and non-clear bags larger than a small clutch are not allowed.

**Key details:**
- These dimensions apply to bags only. They never make an otherwise-prohibited item allowable.
- Full policy: nfl.com/legal/clear-bag-policy.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks about bag rules, backpacks, purses, tote bags, or what size bag they can carry in.

---

### Outside Food, Drinks & Coolers, Not Allowed

**The policy:** Outside food and beverages are not allowed inside NRG Stadium, and coolers are not allowed inside NRG Stadium.

**Key details:**
- Coolers of every kind are prohibited, soft-sided and hard-sided alike. A soft-sided cooler or cooler bag is not permitted even if it would fit within the clear bag dimensions.
- Outside alcohol is not allowed.
- For medically necessary food or drink items, email fanfeedback@houstontexans.com.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks about bringing their own food, snacks, drinks, a water bottle, a cooler, or a soft-sided cooler.

---

### Drones, Strictly Prohibited

**The policy:** Drones, "Unmanned Aircraft Systems," and other remotely controlled aircraft or ground vehicles are strictly prohibited at NRG Stadium and NRG Park.

**Key details:**
- Anyone possessing or operating one is subject to confiscation of the item, removal from NRG Park, and possible legal action.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks about flying a drone, filming from the air, or bringing a remote-controlled device.

---

### Strollers & Car Seats, Checked at Guest Experience

**The policy:** Strollers are allowed in the stadium, but the Houston Texans and NRG Stadium kindly request that guests refrain from bringing them in due to potential tripping hazards. Limited stroller storage is available at the Guest Experience booths, and strollers cannot be brought into the stands. Car seats are allowed but also cannot be brought into the stands and must be checked at a Guest Experience booth.

**Comes up when:** A caller is bringing a baby or toddler and asks about a stroller, a pram, or a car seat.

---

### Permitted Items, What Fans Can Bring

**What's allowed:** Cameras, binoculars, blankets, small umbrellas, iPads, and medicine are all allowed into NRG Stadium. Small cameras with lenses up to 3 inches (3") are permitted after inspection, with no additional or detachable lenses, and camera cases up to 4.5" x 6.5". Binoculars and binocular cases up to 4.5" x 6.5" are allowed after inspection.

**Key details:**
- Computers and iPads; cases must follow the clear bag policy.
- Blankets, if carried alone.
- Small compact umbrellas that fit under a seat, after inspection.
- Medicine, baby wipes, and formula, inside an NFL approved clear bag.
- Full permitted and prohibited list: houstontexans.com/stadium/prohibited-items.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks whether they can bring a camera, a zoom lens, a DSLR, binoculars, a blanket, an umbrella, an iPad, or medication.

---

### Prohibited Items, What Fans Cannot Bring

**What's not allowed:** Noisemakers of every kind are not allowed inside NRG Stadium, including cowbells, bullhorns, air horns, and vuvuzelas. Seat cushions are not allowed. Laser pointers are not allowed. Coolers and outside food and beverages are not allowed.

**Also prohibited:**
- Weapons and firearms.
- Flag poles and sign poles.
- Frisbees and beach balls.
- Selfie sticks.
- Fireworks.
- Illegal substances.
- Battery operated fans.
- Segways, skateboards, and hoverboards.
- Diaper bags (see "Diaper Bags & Baby Items").
- Drones and remote-controlled aircraft.
- Any other item deemed by management, in its sole discretion, to be hazardous or not in the spirit of the event.

**Key details:**
- There is no coat check and no bag storage at the stadium.
- Full list: houstontexans.com/stadium/prohibited-items.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks about an air horn, a cowbell, a noisemaker, a seat cushion, a laser pointer, a selfie stick, a battery fan, or what items are banned.

---

### Diaper Bags & Baby Items, Clear Bag Required

**The policy:** Diaper bags are not permitted inside NRG Stadium. Baby wipes, formula, and diapers may be brought in as long as they are placed inside an NFL approved clear bag. A medically necessary breast pump is allowed in a clear bag.

**Comes up when:** A caller is bringing an infant and asks about a diaper bag, formula, wipes, or a breast pump.

---

### Signs & Banners, Allowed With Conditions

**The policy:** The Houston Texans and NRG Stadium welcome handheld signs and banners, provided they are football-related and in good taste. Signs cannot be commercial, political, religious, or obscene, must be small enough for one person to hold, cannot be affixed to anything, and cannot block sightlines, scoreboards, or permanent signage.

**Key details:**
- Poles are not permitted.
- More info: houstontexans.com/game-day/teammate-central/a-z-guide.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks about bringing a sign, a banner, or a poster to a game.

## Food, Drink & Alcohol

---

### Alcohol Sales, Cutoff After the 3rd Quarter

**The policy:** Alcohol and liquor sales at concession stands stop after the 3rd quarter. You must be 21 or older to buy or consume alcohol in the stadium or in the lots, and outside alcohol is not allowed.

**Key details:**
- Beer concessions are on every level, plus hawkers throughout the aisles serving Bud Light, Budweiser, and Modelo.
- All drinks must be emptied, finished, or thrown away before leaving. Empty souvenir cups may leave.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks about last call, when beer sales stop, what time alcohol sales end, or how late they can buy a drink.

---

### Stadium Bars, Locations

**Key details:**

- Bud Light Cantina — Southwest 100 level behind section 138.
- Bud Light Lounge — North 100 level behind section 116.
- Terrace Bars — sections 103, 123, 129, 518, and 524.
- Each has a DJ pregame and at halftime.

### Gluten Free, Gluten-Avoiding Options

**What's available:** Gluten-avoiding food is available at NRG Stadium. Plain baked potatoes and chopped beef with no sauce are sold at sections 109, 129, 501, 515, 527, and 541. Gluten-avoiding peanuts and cotton candy are sold at all concession stands.

**Key details:**

### Roof Status, Decided Before Kickoff

**The policy:** The roof decision is made 2 hours before kickoff and cannot be changed afterward. Under NFL rules, once the decision is made, the only person who can change it is the Game Referee. Many factors affect it, including chance of rain, cloud cover, and projected wind speed, not just temperature.

**Key details:**
- The roof status for an upcoming game is not known in advance; fans should check back before the next home game.
- With the roof open, a noon kickoff usually shades the west side. This can reverse for later kicks.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks whether the roof will be open or closed, who decides, or when the decision is made.

---

### Smoking & Vaping, Designated Terraces Only

**The policy:** NRG Park is smoke free, including e-cigarettes and vaping, in seating areas and concourses. Smoking is only permitted in the designated smoking terraces, which are on all four sides of the Field Level, on the Club Level, and in each corner of the Upper Level.

**Key details:**
- If one terrace is closed, another on the same level stays open.
- To report smoking near you: text 832-241-0202.

**Comes up when:** A caller asks where they can smoke, whether vaping is allowed, or if there is a smoking section.

### Pets & Service Animals, Service Animals Only

**The policy:** Pets and animals are not permitted in or on NRG Park property. The only exception is certified guide, signal, or service animals trained to aid guests with disabilities. Emotional support animals are not permitted.

### Payment, 100% Cashless With No ATMs

### Texans App & Viewing Tickets

**What to do:** All tickets are mobile and load into your Account Manager and the Texans app once the NFL schedule is released each season, usually around the second week of May. To view tickets in the app: tap MORE, tap LOG IN TO TICKETMASTER, tap CONTINUE and sign in with your Account Manager email, tap the event, then tap VIEW BARCODE.

### Transferring or Forwarding Tickets

**What to do:** In the Texans App, tap MORE, tap LOG IN TO TICKETMASTER, sign in, tap the event, tap TRANSFER, select tickets, tap TRANSFER TO, enter the recipient's contact info, then tap TRANSFER TICKETS.

**Key details:**
- Tickets must be paid in full to transfer or forward. Games pay in schedule order, and greyed-out matchups are unpaid.
- You get an email when tickets are sent and when accepted. Once accepted, your copies are no longer valid.
- To cancel a transfer before it is accepted: open the event and tap CANCEL TRANSFER.
- Help: 832-667-2002, option 1.

**Comes up when:** A caller wants to send tickets to someone, forward them, or cancel a transfer.

---

### Selling or Donating Tickets

**What to do:** To sell tickets in the App or Account Manager: tap your event tickets, select SELL, choose seats, tap SET PRICE (price is per ticket), tap PAYMENT METHOD, choose payout, then tap SELL TICKETS. Tickets must be paid in full to sell.

**Key details:**
- To cancel a sale posting before it sells: tap your event tickets, tap EDIT or REMOVE, select tickets, complete the cancellation.
- To donate a ticket or parking: sign in on a desktop and choose "More" > "Donate." The cutoff is 24 hours before a home game, and donations go to our Cheering Children partner or VetTix.
- Help: 832-667-2002, option 1.

**Comes up when:** A caller wants to resell seats, list tickets, or donate them.

---

### Cannot Attend a Game, Three Options

**The answer:** If you can't attend a game you have three options: transfer or sell your tickets, send them to friends or family, or donate them to a local charity. All three are available in the Official Houston Texans mobile app or Account Manager.

**The options:**
